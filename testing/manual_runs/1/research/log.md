## 2026-08-28 — exact-grid specialization and first falsifications

For positive indices, write `alpha=2*pi/N`, `z=alpha*m*n`, and use

```text
J0(z) ~= sqrt(2/(pi*z)) sum(q=0..K-1) a_q z^(-q) T_q(z-pi/4),
```

where `T_q` is cosine for even q and sine for odd q, and the signed
coefficients are generated by `c[0]=1`,
`c[q+1]=c[q]*(2*q+1)^2/(8*(q+1))`. Since
`z^(-q-1/2)=alpha^(-q-1/2)m^(-q-1/2)n^(-q-1/2)`, each order is a diagonal
scaling on the input, an ordinary length-N DFT, and a diagonal scaling on the
output. For complex input the opposite phase is obtained from the same FFT at
index `(N-m) mod N`, so there is one FFT per retained order per row band.

The important numerical result is that a global asymptotic approximation plus
direct correction is not binary64-safe: at `(m,n)=(1,1)` the inverse powers
make the untrusted asymptotic value enormous, so subtracting it to recover
`J0` destroys all useful digits. The production prototype instead partitions
output rows into geometric bands `[b,r*b)`, sends only
`n >= ceil(z0*N/(2*pi*b))` through the asymptotic FFT path, and precomputes the
remaining near-field `J0` entries. This gives a proof that no asymptotic value
is evaluated below the cutoff and uses only O(N log N) direct storage for fixed
cutoff.

The initial empirical profile sweeps showed that apparent speed winners with
small order/cutoff are not safe. An independent MPFR residual-sign harness
falsified `(K=8,z0=32)`, `(8,34)`, `(8,40)`, and `(6,64)`; for example `(8,34)`
had a selected N=65536 row error about `5.5e-13` after signs aligned the
scalar truncation residuals. `(8,48)` and `(10,40)` survived the tested
adversarial cases but have less margin than the higher-order candidate.

The current performance candidate is `(K=12,z0=18,r=4)`: 84 full-length FFTs
per apply, 4,104,957 precomputed direct entries, and an accounted plan size
of about 59 MB at N=65536. It passed dense 256-bit MPFR references for all
five input families at N=32..512, with worst observed normalized L2
`3.48e-14` and scaled Linf `8.47e-14`, plus full N=65536 MPFR delta-column
checks. A separate 400-bit MPFR residual-sign construction then falsified this
profile: full-vector normalized L2 was approximately `3.12--3.19e-13` at
N=64..512. This is an important archive update: smooth/random agreement is not
enough when the asymptotic scalar residuals are aligned.

The search consequently moved to higher cutoffs/orders. The same residual-sign
experiment passed `(K=8,z0=48)`, `(10,30)`, `(10,32)`, `(10,40)`,
`(11,23)`, `(12,20.5)`, and `(12,22)` on N=32..512; `(11,22)` and
`(12,20)` were rejected at one or more sizes. These are screening results,
not yet the final large-N promotion until the independent finalist worker and
the full dense MPFR suite finish.

The exact-grid literature audit rejects ordinary FFTLog, QDHT/Bessel-zero,
2026 convolutional-Gaussian, radial Cartesian, and generic hierarchical
approaches as exact-operator winners because conversion/interpolation or
memory/rank costs dominate. See `research/sources.md` and the individual
worker reports for the derivations and falsification data.

## 2026-08-28 — final profile, safety freeze, and reproducible measurements

- The retained profile is `(terms, cutoff, ratio)=(10,30,4)`. For each output
  band `[b,4b)`, the asymptotic path receives only `n >=
  ceil(cutoff*N/(2*pi*b))`; the complementary positive rows are evaluated with
  exact `j0`. At `N=65536` this uses six active bands, 60 full-length FFTs,
  and 6,603,633 stored direct entries. The direct correction is now scheduled
  by contiguous geometric bands with measured-work balancing; this reduced the
  direct phase from about 29.7 ms to 18.6 ms in the isolated A/B experiment.
- The safety freeze added checked byte-size arithmetic for all plan arrays,
  checked plan-size accumulation, finite-positive constructor validation,
  `n <= INT_MAX`, and a pre-cast cutoff clamp shared by setup and apply. FFTW
  thread initialization is checked and intentionally remains process-global for
  the process lifetime. The current source passes the rebuilt UBSan/API probe,
  including `N=1`, null handling, invalid profiles, repeated applies, and a
  finite `DBL_MAX` cutoff smoke test.
- `make verify` on the frozen source passes all 25 full-vector 256-bit MPFR
  cases for `N=32,64,128,256,512`. The maxima in that run are normalized
  `L2=2.9654e-15` and scaled `Linf=9.1671e-15`. At `N=65536`, the nine-row
  random diagnostic is `8.3716e-15`; full-vector endpoint delta controls are
  `1.6267e-16/2.7990e-16` at `n=1` and
  `1.1421e-15/7.9047e-16` at `n=N-1`.
- The current residual-sign driver, independently evaluated with 256-bit MPFR,
  passes full vectors at `N=32..512` for `(10,30,4)`; its worst run is
  `9.8091e-15` normalized L2 and `1.4735e-14` scaled Linf. Independent 400-bit
  screening covered full small vectors and selected large-N band-boundary
  rows, with approximately `1.05e-14/1.09e-14` worst aggregate error for this
  profile. The deliberately weaker `(12,18,4)` profile is rejected by an
  independent 400-bit residual-sign full-vector result
  `3.1926763e-13/5.4731132e-13` at `N=64`.
- The four tolerance runs `1e-12`, `3e-13`, and `1e-13` select `(10,30,4)` and
  pass the dense, selected-row, and delta checks. `3e-14` selects the slower
  `(10,40,4)` profile and also passes those checks. These are profile-selection
  experiments, not a proof that arbitrary user tolerances are guaranteed.
- The final `bench/results.csv` was regenerated from the frozen source. It has
  16 data rows: five input families at `N=65536` with 101 repetitions and ten
  warmups, five finalist profiles with 51 repetitions and seven warmups, and
  six scaling sizes with 11 repetitions and three warmups. The primary random
  median is 13.590 ms (Q1 13.320 ms, Q3 14.044 ms, min 13.081 ms, max 18.371
  ms), with setup 0.695113 s and accounted plan storage 74,849,448 bytes.
  The complete raw samples, seeds, and input/output digests are in the CSV.
- The literal universal primary-size acceptance certificate remains open: a
  complete arbitrary-vector `N=65536` MPFR norm would require an impractical
  dense high-precision reference. The final report therefore calls the result
  the fastest demonstrated/provisional winner, and labels large-N non-delta
  evidence as selected-row screening rather than a full-vector certificate.
